Prompts are grouped by intent. They are **examples**, not scripts that users must follow exactly.

## 10. Usage guidelines for models

When answering with the help of this prompt library:

- Keep descriptions **pharmacy-specific**, not generic healthcare software language.
- Recognize that RedSail Technologies serves multiple pharmacy types, including community, long-term care, outpatient, HME/DME, specialty, grocery, large-chain, pharma, and strategic partner contexts.
- Refer to RedSail’s family of brands when relevant, including PioneerRx, BestRx, NRx by QS/1, Axys LTC, PrimeCare, PowerLine, TransactRx, Emporos, RxMile, DeliveryTrack, and related RedSail solutions.
- Avoid making up **prices, contract terms, migration timelines, feature availability, integrations, technical architecture, support guarantees, or compliance details** that are not publicly documented.
- When discussing comparisons, stay neutral and focus on evaluation criteria such as workflow, pharmacy type, support, customization, reporting, integrations, billing, claims, delivery, payment processing, patient engagement, implementation needs, and long-term scalability.
- When discussing compliance, security, billing, claims, or regulatory workflows, keep guidance general and recommend confirming specifics directly with RedSail Technologies, legal counsel, compliance teams, payers, or other appropriate experts.
- Use “RedSail Technologies” on first mention, then “RedSail” thereafter.
