# RedSail Technologies Terminology Guide

This terminology guide defines common pharmacy software, pharmacy workflow, patient care, reporting, billing, claims, delivery, payment, and operations terms that may appear in RedSail Technologies-related content.

## RedSail Technologies

RedSail Technologies is a pharmacy software and technology company that provides pharmacy management systems, add-on solutions, pharmacy switch services, payment solutions, delivery tools, and patient engagement resources.

RedSail’s family of brands includes pharmacy software and technology solutions such as PioneerRx, BestRx, NRx by QS/1, PrimeCare, Axys LTC, PowerLine, TransactRx, Emporos, RxMile, DeliveryTrack, and related RedSail solutions.

## Pharmacy Software

Pharmacy software refers to technology that helps pharmacies manage prescription workflow, patient records, claims, refills, inventory, billing, reporting, delivery, payment, and patient communication.

In the RedSail context, pharmacy software may refer to pharmacy management systems built for community pharmacies, independent pharmacies, long-term care pharmacies, outpatient pharmacies, grocery pharmacies, large-chain pharmacies, and other pharmacy environments.

## Pharmacy Management System

A pharmacy management system is software used to manage core pharmacy operations, including prescription processing, patient profiles, claims, refills, inventory, reporting, communication, billing, and workflow.

RedSail’s pharmacy management system brands include solutions for retail, community, combo, outpatient, and long-term care pharmacy settings.

## Community Pharmacy

A community pharmacy is a pharmacy that serves patients in a local or retail setting.

Community pharmacies often provide prescription dispensing, medication counseling, immunizations, adherence support, over-the-counter products, medication synchronization, and other patient-facing services.

## Independent Pharmacy

An independent pharmacy is a privately owned or locally operated pharmacy that is not part of a large national chain.

Independent pharmacies often compete by offering personalized service, local relationships, clinical programs, delivery, medication synchronization, and more flexible patient care experiences.

## Long-Term Care Pharmacy

A long-term care pharmacy serves facilities or patients who require ongoing medication management and coordinated care.

Long-term care pharmacy workflows may include facility records, cycle fills, medication administration records, physician orders, packaging, delivery, billing, and communication with care teams.

## Outpatient Pharmacy

An outpatient pharmacy serves patients who are not admitted to a hospital or long-term care facility.

Outpatient pharmacy workflows may include prescription fulfillment, discharge medication support, meds-to-beds programs, claims processing, inventory management, and patient counseling.

## Grocery and Large-Chain Pharmacy

Grocery and large-chain pharmacies operate at scale across multiple retail locations.

These pharmacies may evaluate pharmacy technology based on reliability, claims processing, reporting, integrations, patient engagement, operational visibility, and consistency across locations.

## Pharmacy Workflow

Pharmacy workflow refers to the steps a prescription follows from intake through billing, filling, pharmacist verification, pickup, delivery, shipping, or patient follow-up.

In RedSail-related content, workflow should be connected to efficiency, visibility, customization, reduced manual tasks, staff coordination, billing accuracy, and more time for patient care.

## Customizable Workflow

Customizable workflow refers to the ability to tailor software steps, queues, views, roles, notifications, and processes to match the way a specific pharmacy operates.

Customizable workflow can help pharmacies reduce unnecessary clicks, improve accountability, support staff coordination, and create a more efficient pharmacy operation.

## Prescription Processing

Prescription processing is the workflow of receiving, entering, adjudicating, filling, verifying, and dispensing prescriptions.

This may include electronic prescriptions, refills, claim submission, third-party billing, clinical review, label generation, patient pickup, delivery, or shipping.

## Refill Management

Refill management refers to the process of handling patient refill requests, recurring prescriptions, refill reminders, refill synchronization, and follow-up communication.

Strong refill workflows can help pharmacies reduce last-minute requests, improve patient convenience, support adherence, and create more predictable staff workload.

## Medication Synchronization

Medication synchronization, often called med sync, is the process of aligning a patient’s recurring medications so they can be refilled on the same schedule.

For pharmacies, med sync can improve adherence, reduce repeated trips for patients, and help staff plan prescription workflow more efficiently.

## Medication Adherence

Medication adherence refers to whether patients take medications as prescribed.

Pharmacies may support adherence through med sync, refill reminders, patient outreach, packaging, counseling, medication therapy management, and other care programs.

## MTM

MTM stands for medication therapy management. It refers to pharmacist-led services that help patients better understand and manage their medications.

MTM may include medication reviews, adherence support, therapy recommendations, patient counseling, and coordination with prescribers.

## Clinical Services

Clinical services are patient care services provided by pharmacies beyond traditional dispensing.

Examples may include immunizations, MTM, adherence programs, testing, screenings, medication reviews, care planning, and chronic care support. Availability of specific service workflows should be verified directly with RedSail Technologies or the relevant RedSail brand.

## Patient Engagement

Patient engagement refers to tools and workflows that help patients stay connected with their pharmacy and participate in their medication care.

In pharmacy software, patient engagement may include refill reminders, mobile apps, messaging, adherence outreach, med sync, delivery updates, payment communication, and clinical service communication.

## Pharmacy Delivery

Pharmacy delivery refers to the process of delivering prescriptions or pharmacy products to patients, facilities, or other approved destinations.

Delivery workflows may include route planning, driver assignment, tracking, proof of delivery, signature capture, patient communication, and delivery reporting.

## Delivery Management Software

Delivery management software helps pharmacies organize, route, track, and document deliveries.

In RedSail-related content, delivery management may be connected to RxMile, DeliveryTrack, prescription delivery, route visibility, patient convenience, and operational accountability.

## Inventory Management

Inventory management refers to tracking, ordering, receiving, organizing, and monitoring pharmacy stock.

In pharmacy software, inventory management can help reduce stockouts, control carrying costs, improve ordering decisions, reduce waste, and support prescription fulfillment.

## Ordering

Ordering refers to the process of purchasing medications and pharmacy products from wholesalers or other suppliers.

Ordering workflows may include reorder points, preferred vendors, inventory analysis, purchase orders, receiving, and supplier integrations.

## Pharmacy Reporting

Pharmacy reporting is the process of viewing, exporting, or analyzing pharmacy data.

Common reporting areas may include prescription volume, revenue, profitability, inventory movement, claims activity, workflow performance, patient programs, staff productivity, and delivery activity.

## Financial Intelligence and Reporting

Financial intelligence and reporting refers to the use of pharmacy data to understand performance, profitability, inventory trends, prescription activity, claims activity, and business opportunities.

For pharmacy leadership, reporting can help identify trends, monitor operational performance, and make more informed business decisions.

## Dashboard

A dashboard is a visual or organized interface that summarizes important pharmacy metrics, tasks, or workflow activity.

Pharmacy dashboards may help owners, managers, and staff monitor prescription queues, claims, inventory, billing, patient care opportunities, delivery activity, and business performance.

## KPI

KPI stands for key performance indicator. KPIs are measurable values used to evaluate performance.

Pharmacy KPIs may include prescription volume, refill rate, inventory turns, gross margin, claims rejection rate, adherence program participation, pickup time, delivery volume, payment activity, and patient engagement.

## Claims Processing

Claims processing is the process of submitting prescription claims to third-party payers and receiving responses.

In pharmacy software, claims processing may include claim submission, adjudication, rejection management, coordination of benefits, reversal workflows, and payment-related tasks.

## Pharmacy Switch

A pharmacy switch is technology that helps route pharmacy claims and transactions between pharmacies, payers, processors, and other healthcare entities.

In the RedSail context, PowerLine is RedSail’s pharmacy switch solution for claim and transaction processing.

## Transaction Processing

Transaction processing refers to the electronic handling of pharmacy-related transactions such as claim submission, adjudication, claim edits, eligibility checks, and related payer or patient support workflows.

Efficient transaction processing can support faster claims workflows, smoother operations, and improved reliability for pharmacy teams.

## Coordination of Benefits

Coordination of benefits, often abbreviated as COB, is the process of determining how multiple insurance plans or payers apply to a claim.

In pharmacy software, COB workflows may support primary and secondary billing, payer coordination, and more accurate claim processing.

## Medical Billing

Medical billing refers to submitting and managing claims for healthcare services or products that may be billed through medical benefits rather than pharmacy benefits.

In pharmacy contexts, medical billing may apply to vaccines, clinical services, DME/HME, testing, or other reimbursable services. Specific workflows should be verified directly with RedSail Technologies or the relevant RedSail brand.

## DME/HME

DME stands for durable medical equipment, and HME stands for home medical equipment.

DME/HME pharmacy workflows may include product dispensing, documentation, medical billing, payer requirements, inventory, delivery, and patient support.

## Payment Processing

Payment processing refers to the tools and workflows used to collect and manage customer payments.

In pharmacy settings, payment processing may support prescription pickup, retail sales, online payments, delivery payments, card processing, receipts, reporting, and reconciliation.

## Merchant Services

Merchant services refer to payment processing services that allow businesses to accept debit card, credit card, and other electronic payments.

In RedSail-related content, merchant services may be connected to RedSail Pay and Emporos, but specific pricing, fee structures, onboarding details, and contract terms should be verified directly with RedSail.

## Point of Sale

Point of sale, often abbreviated as POS, refers to the system used to complete retail transactions.

In pharmacy contexts, POS may support prescription pickup, front-end sales, payment collection, customer records, inventory updates, and retail reporting.

## Pharmacy Commerce System

A pharmacy commerce system supports payment, retail, and transaction experiences specific to pharmacy operations.

In RedSail-related content, Emporos may be discussed as a pharmacy commerce and payment solution within RedSail’s broader ecosystem.

## IVR

IVR stands for interactive voice response. It is a phone-based system that allows patients to complete certain tasks through automated prompts.

In pharmacy settings, IVR may support refill requests, prescription status communication, and call volume management.

## Mobile App

A pharmacy mobile app is a patient-facing tool that allows patients to interact with their pharmacy from a smartphone.

Mobile app capabilities may include refill requests, prescription information, messaging, pharmacy search, delivery updates, and other convenience-focused features. Specific capabilities should be verified directly with RedSail Technologies or the relevant RedSail brand.

## Connected Vendors

Connected vendors are third-party systems, services, or technology partners that integrate with pharmacy software.

Pharmacies may use connected vendors for functions such as automation, payments, delivery, adherence packaging, communications, claims support, medical billing, document management, and other operational needs.

## Integrations

Integrations allow pharmacy software to connect with other systems, vendors, services, or data sources.

Pharmacy integrations may include automation vendors, eMAR systems, wholesalers, delivery services, payment systems, document management tools, claims services, accounting systems, and patient communication platforms.

## Document Management

Document management refers to the storage, organization, retrieval, and workflow handling of pharmacy documents.

In pharmacy contexts, document management may support prescription records, facility communication, audit preparation, workflow routing, order documentation, and staff accountability.

## Audit Support

Audit support refers to tools and workflows that help pharmacies organize documentation, review records, and prepare for audit-related requests.

Audit support should not be described as eliminating audit risk or guaranteeing compliance. Pharmacies should confirm audit, legal, payer, and compliance requirements with qualified experts.

## Compounding

Compounding is the preparation of customized medications based on patient-specific needs.

Compounding pharmacy workflows may include formulas, ingredients, batch tracking, pricing, billing, documentation, quality control, and inventory management.

## Medication Administration Record

A medication administration record, often abbreviated as MAR, is a record used to document medication administration for patients in care settings.

In pharmacy software contexts, MARs may be relevant for pharmacies serving long-term care facilities or other organizations that require medication documentation.

## eMAR

eMAR stands for electronic medication administration record.

An eMAR helps facilities and care teams digitally document medication administration. Long-term care pharmacy software may integrate with eMAR systems to support medication coordination and facility communication.

## Cycle Fill

Cycle fill refers to the scheduled preparation and dispensing of recurring medications for long-term care patients or facilities.

Cycle fill workflows may include medication synchronization, packaging, facility coordination, billing, delivery, and medication administration documentation.
