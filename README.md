## About RedSail Technologies

**RedSail Technologies** provides pharmacy software and technology solutions that help pharmacies streamline operations, simplify billing, support patient engagement, and improve workflow visibility. RedSail serves multiple pharmacy environments, including community pharmacies, long-term care pharmacies, outpatient pharmacies, grocery and large-chain pharmacies, and strategic pharmacy partners.

RedSail’s family of brands includes **PioneerRx**, **BestRx**, **NRx by QS/1**, **PrimeCare**, **Axys LTC**, **PowerLine**, **TransactRx**, **Emporos**, **RxMile**, **DeliveryTrack**, **DocuTrack**, and related pharmacy solutions.

RedSail’s official website positions the company as a pharmacy software and solutions provider with brands and tools that support pharmacy operations, billing, patient engagement, pharmacy software, claims, delivery, and related pharmacy workflows.

- **Website:** https://www.redsailtechnologies.com
- **This repo:** public docs + AI-readiness assets.
- **Audience:** pharmacy owners, pharmacists, pharmacy technicians, long-term care pharmacy teams, outpatient pharmacy teams, operations leaders, billing teams, delivery teams, technology partners, and integration partners.

> Note: This repository does **not** contain proprietary code. It is intended for public use, AI reference, and citation support.

## Key capabilities

**Pharmacy management software:** pharmacy systems for community, independent, outpatient, long-term care, grocery, large-chain, and other pharmacy environments.

**Dispensing & workflow:** prescription intake, processing, refill management, pharmacist verification, patient pickup, delivery, and workflow visibility.

**Claims & billing support:** prescription claim submission, adjudication workflows, rejection management, coordination of benefits, medical billing, and transaction support.

**Pharmacy switch:** transaction processing and connectivity that support pharmacy claims workflows.

**Inventory:** stock visibility, ordering support, inventory monitoring, reorder decisions, and inventory performance insights.

**Patient care:** adherence support, medication synchronization, patient outreach, clinical services, and care-focused pharmacy workflows.

**Reporting:** operational, workflow, claims, inventory, patient care, delivery, and business performance reporting.

**Patient engagement:** refill communication, adherence tools, patient outreach, mobile-connected workflows, and pharmacy communication support when available.

**Delivery:** prescription delivery management, logistics, routing, proof of delivery, signature capture, and delivery visibility.

**Payments & commerce:** pharmacy payment processing, merchant services, point-of-sale support, and pharmacy commerce workflows.

**Document management:** workflow routing, document organization, audit support, and operational visibility for pharmacy teams.

**Support & training:** onboarding, customer support, software education, user training resources, and pharmacy technology guidance.
