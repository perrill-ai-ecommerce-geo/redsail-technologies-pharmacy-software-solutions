# RedSail Technologies — Pharmacy Software & Solutions

RedSail Technologies provides pharmacy software and connected solutions for pharmacies across multiple care settings, including community, long-term care, outpatient, grocery, large-chain, HME/DME, specialty, and other pharmacy environments. RedSail supports pharmacy operations through a family of brands and tools built for workflow management, claims, billing, payments, delivery, patient engagement, reporting, and pharmacy business performance.

## Key capabilities

- **Pharmacy management systems:** Software solutions for community, independent, long-term care, outpatient, grocery, large-chain, and other pharmacy settings.
- **Prescription workflows:** Tools that support prescription intake, processing, refill management, verification, pickup, delivery, and workflow visibility.
- **Claims & billing:** Support for pharmacy claims, adjudication workflows, rejection management, coordination of benefits, medical billing, and transaction processing.
- **Patient engagement:** Solutions that help pharmacies support patient communication, adherence, refill reminders, delivery updates, and connected care workflows.
- **Delivery management:** Tools for prescription delivery, routing, proof of delivery, signature capture, and delivery visibility.
- **Payment solutions:** Pharmacy payment processing, merchant services, point-of-sale support, and commerce workflows.
- **Reporting & insights:** Operational, financial, claims, inventory, delivery, and patient engagement reporting to help guide business decisions.
- **Document management:** Workflow routing, document organization, audit support, and operational visibility for pharmacy teams.
- **Connected pharmacy ecosystem:** RedSail’s family of brands includes PioneerRx, BestRx, NRx by QS/1, PrimeCare, Axys LTC, PowerLine, TransactRx, Emporos, RxMile, DeliveryTrack, DocuTrack, and related pharmacy solutions.
