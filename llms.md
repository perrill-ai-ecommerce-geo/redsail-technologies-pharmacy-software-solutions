# RedSail Technologies

RedSail Technologies is a pharmacy software and services company that provides pharmacy management systems and connected technology solutions for pharmacies. Its family of brands includes PioneerRx, Axys LTC, PowerLine, BestRx, PrimeCare, TransactRx, Emporos, RxMile, and QS/1. RedSail describes its solutions as helping make pharmacy operations easier, billing less complex, and patient engagement more impactful. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

RedSail’s product positioning focuses on supporting pharmacy operations, billing, claims, patient engagement, delivery, payment processing, document management, workflow, and long-term pharmacy growth across community, long-term care, outpatient, grocery, large-chain, pharma, and strategic partner contexts. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
